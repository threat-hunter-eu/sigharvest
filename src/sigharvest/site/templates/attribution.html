Attribution and licences

The rules on this site were written by the authors of the repositories below and keep their licences. Each rule page names the rule's licence (the rule's own license: field, else the licence of the repository its canonical copy comes from) and links to the canonical copy.

The rule text is republished only for these licence classes: {{ classes|join(', ') or 'none' }}. Rules without a stated licence are listed with their metadata and a link to their source only: without a licence nobody else may redistribute the text.

Licence classes are guidance encoded as code, not legal advice. If you are an author and want a rule removed or attributed differently, open an issue in the sigharvest repository{% if contact_email %} or write to {{ contact_email }}{% endif %}.

Machine-readable copy: ATTRIBUTION.md.{% if has_notice %} Copyright notices of the sources: NOTICE.md.{% endif %} Each release also ships LICENSES.zip with the full licence texts and the publishers' own licence files. Copies of SigmaHQ rules published elsewhere under another licence are marked on their rule page and keep the Detection Rule License attribution.
